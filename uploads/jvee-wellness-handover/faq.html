Booking & Sessions

Do I need to be a member to book a session?

No. First-time guests book directly through single sessions or packages on the Contact page. Membership becomes worthwhile once you know which goal track fits your routine.

How far in advance should I book?

Public booking typically fills 5–7 days out. Practice and Executive members book inside that window, including same-week and 24-hour guaranteed slots.

What is your cancellation policy?

Sessions can be rescheduled free of charge up to 24 hours in advance. Cancellations inside 24 hours are charged 50% of the session rate.

Do you accommodate specific medical conditions?

Your intake consult includes a health history review. Practitioners adjust or decline specific protocols based on contraindications before any session is booked.

Memberships & Packages

Do unused session credits expire?

No. Membership credits roll forward month to month for as long as your membership stays active.

Can I pause or cancel my membership?

Yes. Memberships can be paused for up to two months per year, or cancelled with 30 days' notice, through your practitioner or the contact page.

What's the difference between a membership and a package?

Memberships are monthly and ongoing, with priority booking and a continuity practitioner. Packages are one-time, prepaid session bundles with no recurring commitment.

Corporate & Gift Cards

Can my company set up a corporate program?

Yes. Our corporate wellness program covers on-site recovery days, executive membership bundles, and quarterly team resets. Reach out through the contact page to scope a plan for your team.

Do gift cards expire?

No. JVEE gift cards never expire and can be applied to any treatment, package, or membership.
