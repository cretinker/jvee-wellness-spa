Questions

Before you book.

Do I need to be a member to book a session?

No. First-time guests book directly through single sessions or packages. Membership is available once you know which goal track fits your routine.

How is JVEE different from a typical day spa?

We build every plan around a measured outcome and a continuity practitioner, using clinical-grade diagnostics rather than a fixed treatment menu.

Can my company set up a corporate program?

Yes — our corporate wellness program covers on-site days, executive membership bundles, and quarterly team resets. Reach out through the contact page to scope a plan.

What is your cancellation policy?

Sessions can be rescheduled free of charge up to 24 hours in advance. See our full policy on the FAQ page.
