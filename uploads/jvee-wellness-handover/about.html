Our Story

Built for athletes. Kept open for anyone in Lagos serious about how they feel.

JVEE began in 2012 as a private recovery lab for a handful of professional athletes in Lagos. The protocols worked well enough that their families, then their colleagues, asked for a way in. Fourteen years later, the clinical rigor hasn't changed — only who it's for.

Wellness Timeline

Fourteen years, one standard.

2012

The Lab

Dr. Chukwu opens a private recovery practice for three professional athletes and a waiting list of one.

2016

The Skin Bar

Kunle Adebayo joins to build a clinical skin program rooted in diagnostics rather than trends.

2020

Membership Opens

The practice formalizes into three membership tiers to serve a growing, non-athlete client base.

2024

Corporate Program

JVEE launches on-site and executive wellness programs for teams who wanted the same rigor at work.

The Facility

Every room is built around one protocol.

No shared treatment rooms, no generic massage tables. Each space exists for a single category of work.
