Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 1, 2026

JVEE Wellness ("JVEE," "we," "us") respects your privacy and the sensitivity of the health information you share with us. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how it's protected.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you provide when booking, subscribing, or enquiring — name, email, phone number.
  • Appointment details — preferred goal, treatment, date, and time.
  • Health information you disclose during intake consults and treatment history, used solely to deliver safe, personalized care.
  • Payment information, processed by our payment provider — JVEE does not store full card numbers.

How we use it

  • To schedule, confirm, and deliver your appointments and memberships.
  • To personalize treatment protocols based on your intake and progress reviews.
  • To send appointment reminders, membership updates, and — only if you opt in — the Wellness Journal newsletter.
  • To administer corporate wellness programs, limited to information the sponsoring organization is authorized to receive.

How we protect it

Health and treatment records are stored separately from marketing and billing data, with access limited to your assigned practitioners and care team. We do not sell personal information to third parties.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe from the Wellness Journal at any time via the link in each email.
  • Request a copy of, correction to, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us.
  • Decline to share specific health details — your practitioner will advise if this limits which protocols we can safely offer.

Contact us

Questions about this policy can be directed to our contact page or hello@jveewellnessspa.com.
